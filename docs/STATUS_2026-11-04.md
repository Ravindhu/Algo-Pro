  `docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` is now outdated.

## 7. MAE / MFE in the optimizer (changed today, UNTESTED weights)
- `Blue_engine/backtest/engine.py`: each closed trade now records `mae` and
  `mfe` (fractions of entry price). The entry bar's own range is excluded.
- `Blue_engine/backtest/strategy.py`: passes per-trade return, P&L, MAE and MFE
  to the optimizer.
- `Blue_engine/optimizer/objective.py`: score is now
  `mean - 0.5*std - 0.02*(mean MAE in %) + 0.1*(mean MFE capture)`.
  With no trade data the score is unchanged.
- Both weights (`MAE_PENALTY_PER_PCT`, `MFE_CAPTURE_WEIGHT`) are hand-set
  defaults. They are not validated and must be tuned on out-of-sample data.
- MAE/MFE are NOT shown on the dashboard.
- Helpers checked by hand. Full engine and optimizer tests need `openalgo`.
